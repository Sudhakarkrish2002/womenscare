Mind & Body Harmony

Mental Wellness & Menstrual Health

Learn how fluctuating hormones influence moods, energy, and stress — and embrace gentle restorative yoga for profound comfort.

Understanding the Mind-Body Connection

The brain and ovaries continuously communicate through neurohormonal feedback loops. During the luteal phase (the days preceding bleeding), estrogen and progesterone drop rapidly, impacting neurotransmitters like serotonin and dopamine.

Recognizing that mood changes, brain fog, and sensitivity are biological responses rather than personal shortcomings helps eliminate self-blame and encourages compassionate self-care.

Common Emotional Fluctuations

Familiar cycle-linked experiences and evidence-based coping strategies.

Premenstrual Syndrome (PMS)

Mild to moderate mood swings, irritability, or tearfulness 3–7 days before menstruation. Regular sleep, reduced caffeine, and light walks help steady mood.

Premenstrual Dysphoric Disorder

A clinically recognized condition causing severe depression, intense anger, and disabling anxiety. If symptoms impair work or life, speak with a doctor.

Cycle-Related Anxiety

Heightened nervous tension coinciding with luteal progesterone decline. Slow 4-7-8 breathing and mindful journaling restore groundedness.

Menstrual Fatigue

Heavy physical tiredness due to metabolic changes and iron loss. Permitting yourself to slow down and take afternoon rest aids cellular recovery.

Yoga Poses for Menstrual Comfort

Gentle, floor-based postures that decompress the lower spine and soothe cramps.

Pose 01
Relaxing Pain Relief

Child's Pose (Balasana)

Kneel on your mat with knees wide, sit back on your heels, and fold forward with arms extended. Gently releases tension in the lower back and pelvic floor.

Pose 02
Hip Opening Circulation

Butterfly Pose (Baddha Konasana)

Sit tall, bring the soles of your feet together, and let your knees relax outward. Encourages pelvic circulation and eases inner thigh cramp tightness.

Pose 03
Spinal Health Digestive

Cat-Cow (Marjaryasana-Bitilasana)

On all fours, inhale as you arch your spine and look gently up, then exhale as you round your back. Mobilizes the lumbar spine and reduces bloating.

Pose 04
Restorative Stress Relief

Reclining Bound Angle Pose

Lie comfortably on your back with a pillow under your knees and soles together. Place one hand on your heart and one on your belly for calming breath.

Pose 05
Venous Drainage Anti-Fatigue

Legs-Up-The-Wall (Viparita Karani)

Extend your legs upward against a wall while resting your upper body on a soft mat. Drains tired legs, soothes backaches, and cools the nervous system.
