Nutritional Guidance

Food & Nutrition During Menstruation

Fuel your body with the right minerals, healthy fats, and hydrating foods to ease cramps and stabilize energy levels.

Nutritional Support for Your Cycle

During menstruation, your body undergoes natural hormonal shifts while losing vital micronutrients like iron. Eating balanced, nutrient-dense meals can significantly reduce uterine inflammation, ease muscle spasms, and maintain steady blood sugar.

Did you know? Magnesium and Omega-3 fatty acids actively inhibit the production of inflammatory prostaglandins — the natural hormone-like chemicals responsible for painful menstrual contractions.

Essential Menstrual Nutrients

Targeted nutritional powerhouses that replenish what your body sheds.

Iron-Rich Foods

Replenish iron levels lost through menstrual bleeding to prevent fatigue, weakness, and anemia.

  • Spinach, kale & dark leafy greens
  • Lentils, chickpeas & black beans
  • Lean meats & poultry
  • Pumpkin seeds & sesame seeds
  • Tofu and fortified soy foods

Magnesium Sources

Relaxes uterine muscle tension, reduces fluid retention, and calms premenstrual headaches.

  • Dark chocolate (70%+ cacao)
  • Avocados & bananas
  • Almonds, cashews & walnuts
  • Quinoa, brown rice & oats
  • Chia seeds & sunflower seeds

Omega-3 Fatty Acids

Natural anti-inflammatory properties that reduce cramps and ease lower back stiffness.

  • Wild salmon, sardines & mackerel
  • Ground flaxseeds & flaxseed oil
  • Walnuts & chia pudding
  • Hemp seeds
  • Algal oil (plant-based omega-3)

Foods to Include Generously

Incorporate these nourishing options into breakfast, lunch, and warm dinners:

  • Hydrating fruits: Watermelon, cucumber, berries, and oranges replenish electrolytes.
  • Warm herbal infusions: Ginger tea, chamomile, and peppermint help soothe intestinal cramps.
  • Complex carbohydrates: Sweet potatoes, whole grain toast, and oatmeal prevent mood dips.

Foods to Limit During Periods

These foods frequently exacerbate bloating, breast tenderness, and mood swings:

  • Excessive caffeine: High coffee intake constricts blood vessels and worsens cramps and anxiety.
  • High sodium snacks: Chips and processed soups drive severe water retention and abdominal bloating.
  • Refined sugars: Cause rapid glucose spikes followed by sharp crashes, amplifying irritability.
