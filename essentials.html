Menstrual Cup

Eco-Friendly

Made of medical-grade silicone that collects menstrual fluid. It offers 10–12 hours of leak protection and lasts for up to 5–10 years.

  • Up to 12 hours of continuous wear
  • Reusable & cost-effective long-term
  • Requires boiling for sterilization
  • Safe for swimming and sports

Sanitary Pads

Most Popular

Soft, absorbent cotton pads worn in underwear to catch menstrual flow. Available in various lengths with wings for daytime and overnight security.

  • Change every 4–6 hours
  • No insertion required, beginner-friendly
  • Available in multiple absorbency sizes
  • Organic cotton options prevent rashes

Tampons

Active Life

Small cylindrical absorbent products inserted internally into the vagina. Great for athletes, gym sessions, and swimming.

  • Change strictly every 4–8 hours
  • Never wear for more than 8 hours
  • Choose lowest absorbency needed
  • Helps avoid TSS (Toxic Shock Syndrome)

Cloth Pads

Zero Waste

Breathable, chemical-free washable cloth pads crafted from organic cotton layers. Reduces landfill waste and skin chafing.

  • Machine or hand washable with cold water
  • Lasts up to 2–3 years with care
  • Gentle on sensitive skin

  • Drug-free, immediate pain relief

  • Great for abdominal and lower back cramps
