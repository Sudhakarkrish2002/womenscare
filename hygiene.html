Menstrual Hygiene Guidelines

Essential hygiene recommendations from leading health authorities to prevent urogenital infections, odor, and irritation.

Hand Washing Protocol

Wash hands with soap and running water before and immediately after handling any menstrual products, and after using the restroom.

  • Scrub palms, fingers, and under nails for 20 seconds
  • Dry hands completely with a clean towel
  • Use 60%+ alcohol hand sanitizer if water is unavailable

Changing Frequency

Changing products consistently prevents bacterial growth, contact dermatitis, and foul odors from trapped moisture.

  • Pads: Every 4 to 6 hours
  • Tampons: Every 4 to 8 hours
  • Menstrual Cups: Every 8 to 12 hours
  • Cloth Pads: Same frequency as disposable pads

Sanitary Disposal

Dispose of products safely to protect communal plumbing, public sanitation, and local environmental health.

  • Wrap used products in toilet tissue or original wrapper
  • Place in dedicated sanitary bins
  • Never flush pads, tampons, or wet wipes down toilets

Daily Personal Hygiene Routine

Simple, gentle hygiene habits for comfort and natural balance.

Daily Showering

Bathing daily washes away dried blood, keeps intimate skin refreshed, and relaxes cramping muscles.

Front to Back Wiping

Always wipe from front to back after using the toilet to avoid transferring bacteria from the bowel to the urethra.

Cotton Underwear

Wear breathable, 100% cotton underwear. Avoid tight synthetic thongs that trap heat and moisture.

No Harsh Scents

Avoid scented pads or genital deodorants. The vagina is self-cleaning; perfumes disrupt natural lactobacilli flora.

Tampon Safety & Toxic Shock Syndrome (TSS)

Toxic Shock Syndrome is a rare but life-threatening bacterial condition caused by Staphylococcus aureus. Following these strict guidelines minimizes your risk:

  • Always use the lowest absorbency necessary for your flow rate.
  • Never wear a single tampon for longer than 8 hours under any circumstances.
  • Alternate tampons with sanitary pads, particularly overnight while sleeping.
  • If you develop sudden high fever, rash, dizziness, or vomiting, remove the tampon and seek emergency care.
