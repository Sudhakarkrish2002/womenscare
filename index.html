Understanding Menstruation

A natural biological cycle experienced by millions around the world.

Introduction: The purpose of this project is to provide reliable, medically aligned information and practical support during menstruation, focusing on hygiene, nutrition, and comforting lifestyle remedies.

WOmenCare consists of user-friendly sections providing both physical and emotional health guidance. This platform bridges modern technology with holistic care to make menstrual awareness accessible to all.

Menstruation (a period) is the monthly shedding of the uterine lining when pregnancy does not occur. Good hygiene prevents infections, reduces odor, and supports maximum comfort throughout your monthly rhythm.

Quick Health Tips

Simple daily habits that make a noticeable difference.

Stay Hydrated

Drink at least 2 liters of water daily to reduce fluid retention, headaches, and fatigue.

Practice Good Hygiene

Change pads every 4-6 hours and wash hands thoroughly before and after handling products.

Listen to Your Body

Rest when fatigued, use a warm compress for cramps, and avoid pushing through severe pain.

Eat Balanced Meals

Incorporate spinach, lentils, seeds, and leafy greens to replenish iron and magnesium.
